Bio

I'm an AI researcher on the founding team of Vetto AI, where I build benchmarks and datasets to evaluate and train frontier models. Lately I've been focused on synthetic and human-augmented data for AI for Science.

Before that, I spent almost five years at Nubank. I started as a machine learning engineer and ended up leading a team of nine engineers who built the company's internal data, ML and AI infrastructure. I hold an M.Sc. in Software Engineering from the University of Alberta and a B.Sc. in Computer Science from the Federal University of ABC (UFABC) in Brazil.
